## Recording standard

- Video master: 1080p, 30 fps.
- Use overhead close-ups for paper, billets, secret-writing practice and prop handling.
- Audio: clean spoken recording with no unlicensed music.
- Every participant must understand that the recording is an entertainment/education demonstration and separately consent to publication.
- Never display passwords, financial data, medical information, grief-related material or other private information.
- Do not reconstruct or expose proprietary commercial gimmicks, routines or scripts.

## Content and attribution rules

- All app prose, scripts, diagrams, artwork and routine combinations should be original.
- Historical principles should be credited where known.
- Research may use legitimate books and purchased instruction, but do not copy passages, exact commercial scripts, proprietary gimmick construction, marked systems, book-test layouts or branded routines.
- When inspired by a commercial effect, reproduce only the broad audience-facing effect using an independently designed method and presentation.

## Release acceptance criteria

- All 45 lessons open correctly and are ordered Mentalism → Hypnosis → Magic.
- Search, bookmarks, completion and quizzes persist offline.
- Existing Mentalism completion/bookmarks migrate into the new Arcana storage keys.
- Hypnosis lessons clearly preserve consent, safety, cancellation and non-clinical boundaries.
- No participant private information appears in release media.
- Artwork, music and footage are original, licensed or public domain with records retained.
- At least three external testers can follow lesson procedures without verbal clarification.
- Android production build installs, resumes and preserves progress on a physical low-memory device.
- Final Play Store listing clearly states: one purchase, complete course, no ads, no subscription.
